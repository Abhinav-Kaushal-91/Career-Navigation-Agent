# User Journeys

## 1. Student Exploration

- **Input:** Education, Python coursework, two projects, a hackathon, customer-service work, and preferences.
- **Steps:** Extract evidence; separate academic from applied/production work; search entry-level, junior, associate, internship, co-op, and graduate roles; classify paths; identify evidence gaps.
- **Decision points:** Explore several role families or focus on one; accept a recommended action.
- **Approval points:** Profile facts, inferred capabilities, target/preferences, and final plan.
- **Failure branches:** Resume parsing failure, sparse profile, no exact results, insufficient evidence, or search timeout.
- **Final output:** Limited explainable role options, evidence gaps, actions, sources, confidence, and limitations.

## 2. Senior UiPath Market Opportunity Analysis

- **Input:** Senior UiPath Developer profile and geography.
- **Steps:** Search exact and related titles; normalize role family; count verified postings and employers; analyze locations, recurring requirements, and stringency; assess broader transferability.
- **Decision points:** Exact title versus related role exploration.
- **Approval points:** Confirm profile and inferred capabilities.
- **Failure branches:** Duplicates, stale postings, inaccessible pages, or too few postings for stringency.
- **Final output:** Separate market verdict and candidate verdict with evidence.

## 3. Product Owner Transition

- **Input:** Profile, Product Owner goal, preferences, and timeline.
- **Steps:** Analyze Product Owner and related titles; compare confirmed capabilities; identify transferable and product-specific gaps; test direct application and bridge options.
- **Decision points:** Apply directly, pursue a bridge such as Technical Product Owner only when supported, or return insufficient evidence.
- **Approval points:** Target and inferred transferability; final plan.
- **Failure branches:** Ambiguous target, no credible bridge, or insufficient market history.
- **Final output:** Market snapshot, accessibility verdict, gap explanation, and path.

## 4. AI Solutions Architect Transition

- **Input:** Senior RPA Developer profile and two-year target.
- **Steps:** Confirm profile; analyze target market; compare architecture, AI, leadership, and evidence requirements; search bridges; assess timeline; create staged roadmap.
- **Decision points:** Whether an intermediate role is required and whether the timeline is aggressive but plausible.
- **Approval points:** Profile, capabilities, target, and final plan.
- **Failure branches:** Missing prerequisites, unavailable history, search failure, or no credible bridge.
- **Final output:** Assumptions, risks, confidence, sources, gaps, bridge, milestones, and reassessment triggers.

## 5. Director Progression

- **Input:** Current role, intended Director function and context, scope evidence, and two-year timeline.
- **Steps:** Analyze Director postings; normalize seniority; evaluate people, portfolio, budget, vendors, strategy, executive communication, influence, scope, and outcomes.
- **Decision points:** Direct target versus Senior Manager, Lead, Head of Function, or another intermediate target.
- **Approval points:** Intended function/context, facts, inferred scope, target, and final plan.
- **Failure branches:** Ambiguous function, insufficient postings, unrealistic timeline, or unconfirmed prerequisite.
- **Final output:** Non-guaranteed progression assessment, scope gaps, milestones, risks, and evidence.

## 6. Open Career Exploration

- **Input:** Background, transferable skills, interests, and constraints without a fixed target.
- **Steps:** Build capability clusters; search multiple plausible role families; return a limited explainable set.
- **Decision points:** Reject, explore, or refine a pathway.
- **Approval points:** Profile, inferred capabilities, selected path, and final plan.
- **Failure branches:** Sparse evidence, search failure, or no sufficiently supported paths.
- **Final output:** A small set of role directions with tradeoffs and next actions.

## 7. Career Reassessment

- **Input:** Updated profile, such as a completed MCP and LangGraph project, plus prior confirmed profile and plan.
- **Steps:** Compare versions; preserve assumptions; refresh market evidence; recalculate gaps and readiness; explain changes.
- **Decision points:** Keep, revise, reject, or explore a path.
- **Approval points:** Updated facts, inferred capability, target, and revised plan.
- **Failure branches:** Cannot compare versions, market unavailable, or evidence remains insufficient.
- **Final output:** Traceable change report and revised plan only after approval.
