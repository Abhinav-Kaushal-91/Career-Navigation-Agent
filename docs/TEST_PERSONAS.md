# MVP Test Personas

These are synthetic personas for MVP validation and contain no real personal information.

## Persona A: Student

A computer science student with Python coursework, two projects, one hackathon, part-time customer-service experience, no full-time professional technology experience, and open exploration across entry-level technology roles.

**Expected test focus:** Academic versus production evidence, no formal employment support, entry-level search, transferable skills, and a complete reviewable workflow.

## Persona B: Career Transition

A Senior UiPath Developer with RPA delivery experience, API integrations, stakeholder collaboration, production support, automation design, and a goal of becoming an AI Solutions Architect within 24 months.

**Expected test focus:** Transferability, exact versus related titles, architecture and AI gaps, bridge-role analysis, market evidence, and timeline realism.

## Persona C: Leadership Progression

A senior technical professional with strong individual-contributor experience, some project leadership, and limited confirmed people, budget, portfolio, and strategy ownership, with a goal of becoming a Director within 24 months.

**Expected test focus:** Technical seniority versus leadership scope, title ambiguity, intermediate roles, scope gaps, and non-guaranteed progression assessment.
