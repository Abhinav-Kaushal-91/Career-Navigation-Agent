# Product Scope

## MVP Features

- Resume upload and manual profile input, including users without formal employment.
- Structured profile extraction, correction, confirmation, and persistent saving.
- Support for every career stage, including open exploration and reassessment.
- Target role, seniority, timeline, location, work mode, industry, preferences, and exclusions.
- Current market analysis using exact and related titles, selected full-content retrieval, deduplication, title normalization, employer counts, requirement extraction, and limitations.
- Historical market analysis only when credible evidence exists.
- Direct-fit, adjacent, bridge, aspirational, poor-fit, and insufficient-evidence classifications.
- Transferable skills and exposure, demonstrated, applied, production, and leadership maturity.
- Skill, experience, leadership/scope, evidence, credential/prerequisite, and hard-blocker analysis.
- Separate market verdict and candidate-accessibility verdict.
- Timeline feasibility, phased roadmap, milestones, sources, confidence, uncertainty, graceful failure handling, and human approval.
- Persistent saving of the confirmed profile and approved plan.

## Non-goals for the MVP

- Automatic applications, ATS form filling, browser automation, recruiter outreach, or email sending.
- Resume tailoring, cover-letter generation, interview preparation, or salary negotiation.
- Voice interface, course marketplace, learning-management system, or social networking.
- Multiple-user authentication, enterprise multi-tenancy, exact applicant-count prediction, guaranteed market coverage, or offer probability.
- Pinecone implementation, scheduled n8n refresh, ElevenLabs, or Lyzr workflow implementation.

## Later Extensions

Potential later work includes weekly market snapshots, exact-title historical collection, scheduled alerts, salary intelligence, broader international support, richer integrations, and workflow automation. Each requires explicit scope, evidence, and architecture decisions.
