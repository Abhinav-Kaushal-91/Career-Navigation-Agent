# Personas

## Student or Recent Graduate

- **Background:** May have education, coursework, projects, hackathons, internships, co-op, volunteering, part-time work, and portfolio evidence, but little or no full-time technology experience.
- **Goals:** Find realistic entry points, translate learning into evidence, explore directions, and identify missing evidence.
- **Frustrations:** Job titles are confusing; academic work is often undervalued or overstated.
- **Inputs:** Education, coursework, projects, internships, activities, skills, preferences, and target timeline.
- **Desired outputs:** Direct-entry roles, adjacent paths, evidence gaps, and practical employability actions.
- **Risks:** Academic exposure may be misrepresented as production experience.
- **Relevant evidence types:** Coursework, projects, hackathons, internships, co-op, volunteering, part-time work, student leadership, portfolio, and GitHub.

## Early-Career Professional

- **Background:** Has initial applied work experience and is choosing a specialization or a stronger second or third role.
- **Goals:** Understand adjacent options and build credible evidence for progression.
- **Frustrations:** Generic advice does not distinguish potential from applied experience.
- **Inputs:** Employment history, achievements, skills, projects, preferences, and target roles.
- **Desired outputs:** Targetable roles, specialization options, gaps, and evidence-building milestones.
- **Risks:** Aspirational roles may be recommended without enough applied experience.
- **Relevant evidence types:** Employment outcomes, projects, certifications, stakeholder work, metrics, and references.

## Mid-Career Transitioner

- **Background:** Has substantial experience in one role family and wants to move into another.
- **Goals:** Identify transferable skills, the strongest bridge role, and whether retraining or role experience is needed.
- **Frustrations:** Keyword matching misses capability transfer and overweights titles.
- **Inputs:** Career history, delivery outcomes, skills, preferences, target role, and timeline.
- **Desired outputs:** Transferability analysis, bridge options, gap types, and a realistic sequence.
- **Risks:** The system may underestimate transferable capabilities or ignore prerequisite blockers.
- **Relevant evidence types:** Production delivery, architecture, integrations, stakeholder work, outcomes, projects, and leadership scope.

## Senior Professional Pursuing Leadership

- **Background:** Strong technical or functional contributor who wants Senior Manager, Head of Function, or Director scope.
- **Goals:** Understand leadership expectations, missing organizational evidence, and timeline realism.
- **Frustrations:** Technical seniority is often treated as equivalent to leadership scope.
- **Inputs:** People leadership, portfolio, budget, vendors, strategy, executive communication, and outcomes.
- **Desired outputs:** Scope gaps, realistic intermediate roles, milestones, and risks without promotion guarantees.
- **Risks:** Seniority, certifications, or project leadership may be mistaken for people or portfolio leadership.
- **Relevant evidence types:** Team size, hiring, performance management, budgets, vendors, strategy, executive influence, and measurable outcomes.

## Career Explorer or Returnee

- **Background:** Is uncertain about direction or is returning after a voluntarily supplied career break.
- **Goals:** Explore credible directions, understand current opportunity, and identify relevant previous skills.
- **Frustrations:** Stale profiles and unexplained career gaps can distort recommendations.
- **Inputs:** Prior experience, interests, constraints, preferences, break context when supplied, and updated evidence.
- **Desired outputs:** A limited set of explainable paths, re-entry actions, and reassessment triggers.
- **Risks:** The system may unfairly penalize a career break or use stale information.
- **Relevant evidence types:** Prior work, volunteer work, projects, recent learning, portfolio evidence, and user-confirmed preferences.
