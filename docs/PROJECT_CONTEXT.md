# Project Context

## Purpose

AI Career Strategy & Market Navigator is a market-grounded career intelligence and career strategy agent. It helps people understand which roles they can realistically target and build an evidence-supported path toward a chosen goal.

## Users

The product supports students, recent graduates, early-career professionals, mid-career professionals, career transitioners, senior professionals pursuing leadership, people returning after a career break, and people uncertain about their next direction. Career stage changes analysis but does not determine eligibility.

## Use Cases

1. **Career exploration:** identify realistic direct-fit and adjacent roles when the user has no fixed target.
2. **Career transition:** identify transferable capabilities, bridge roles, gaps, and a realistic path to an aspirational role.
3. **Career progression:** assess leadership, scope, evidence, and timeline requirements for a more senior role.
4. **Market opportunity intelligence:** analyze current exact-title and related-title opportunities, employers, requirements, and evidence quality.
5. **Career reassessment:** compare an updated profile and market with a prior approved plan and explain what changed.

## Expected End Result

The product should provide direct-fit, adjacent, bridge, aspirational, poor-fit, or insufficient-evidence roles; market and candidate verdicts; skill, experience, leadership/scope, evidence, and prerequisite gaps; timeline feasibility; a staged roadmap; sources and retrieval dates; confidence; uncertainty; and human approval before inferred information or the final plan is saved. It uses current market evidence and credible historical market evidence when available.

Authoritative definitions are maintained in the documents referenced by `AGENTS.md`.
