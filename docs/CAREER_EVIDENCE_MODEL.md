# Career Evidence Model

## Evidence Categories

- **Confirmed facts:** User-provided or user-corrected education, dates, employers, titles, projects, skills, leadership, and other profile facts.
- **User preferences:** Desired roles, seniority, timeline, location, work mode, industry, bridge-role willingness, exclusions, and other constraints.
- **Inferred capabilities:** Agent hypotheses derived from confirmed evidence; they remain inferred until approved.
- **Market evidence:** Retrieved postings, source metadata, observed requirements, counts, role-family mappings, and historical observations.
- **Agent interpretations:** Explanations, classifications, comparisons, feasibility judgments, and recommendations derived from the preceding categories.

## Evidence Maturity

1. **Exposure:** Encountered or studied a concept.
2. **Demonstrated:** Produced project or portfolio evidence.
3. **Applied:** Used it in internship, freelance, volunteer, or limited work.
4. **Production:** Used it in a live business or operational environment.
5. **Leadership:** Led people, decisions, architecture, delivery, or outcomes involving it.

These levels are not equivalent. Academic exposure with project evidence is not confirmed production experience.

## Gap Categories

- **Skill gap:** Missing knowledge or technical capability.
- **Experience gap:** Capability may exist, but appropriate applied evidence is missing.
- **Leadership/scope gap:** Missing people, portfolio, budget, vendor, strategy, architecture ownership, executive influence, cross-functional authority, or organizational/geographic scope.
- **Evidence gap:** Capability is not demonstrated through outcomes, metrics, deliverables, achievements, references, cases, or portfolio evidence.
- **Credential/prerequisite gap:** Missing degree, certification, license, clearance, authorization, language, or other formal prerequisite.
- **Hard blocker:** A mandatory requirement that materially prevents immediate eligibility.

## Approval Rules

The user confirms or corrects extracted profile facts before they are saved. Inferred capabilities remain marked inferred until explicit approval. The user confirms target role, seniority, timeline, location, industry, and bridge-role willingness. A generated plan is not the active saved plan until final user approval.
